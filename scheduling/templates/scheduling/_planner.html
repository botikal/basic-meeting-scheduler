Without it, clicking ahead of a slow response (real risk - see the timing notes around this template's usage) fires overlapping requests all targeting this same element, and whichever happens to finish last wins the swap regardless of click order - clicks feel like they don't register, or land on the wrong state. "this:replace" cancels the in-flight request the moment a newer one starts, so the UI always reflects the most recent click. {% endcomment %}
